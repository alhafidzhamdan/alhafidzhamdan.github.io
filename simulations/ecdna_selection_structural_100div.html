Twice the circle mis-repairs: an intramolecular rearrangement is seeded at division 20 and again at division 50, spanning every SV class an episome can make — deletion, duplication, and both inversion junctions. There is no translocation: a lone circle has nothing to translocate to. Selection then sweeps each rearranged, higher-dosage variant through the population.

Reading it: the flat red track is total copy number — chromosomal background plus k × (per-copy content) — climbing as selection amplifies the circle. Arcs are the ecDNA's own junctions: the ever-present founder SV — the circularisation join (highlighted in every panel), then at division 20 a tandem DUP over EGFR (raising its dosage) and a DEL of a passenger flank (the dip to CN 1), and at division 50 an inversion adding paired h2h / t2t junctions. Each rearrangement that lifts EGFR dosage is then swept to fixation by selection. Arc height is read support (right axis, shared across panels): the founder junction rides on every copy, so its read support tracks the amplicon's copy number — climbing each division to a maximum at division 100, marked by the amber guide. From division 2 the excision scar appears — the chromosomal deletion left behind when the segment looped out — drawn below the baseline at low, flat read support: it stays on the chromosome, un-amplified, so it never climbs like the founder junction. That contrast (high, rising circle junction vs low, flat scar) is the episomal-ecDNA signature EpiTracer reads.
