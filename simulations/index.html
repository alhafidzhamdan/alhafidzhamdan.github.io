Extrachromosomal DNA · interactive

ecDNA simulation

Three browser-based models of how an ecDNA is born and evolves — acentric circles carrying oncogenes, amplified and reshaped over cell divisions, formed by two different mechanisms (episomal excision and micronuclear chromothripsis). Built to accompany EpiTracer.

Neutral · 100 divisions

Birth & neutral drift

One ecDNA excised in the founder, then replication + random (acentric) segregation with no selection. Watch the copy-number distribution spread and the amplicon drift toward loss — with transient amplification along the way.

Open simulation → Selection + rearrangements · 100 divisions

Selection & structural evolution

Positive selection on oncogene dosage, with intramolecular rearrangements seeded at divisions 20 and 50 (DUP, DEL, inversion — no translocation). Structural variants sweep the population; genome profiles are drawn plot_sv_linear-style with the founder circularisation SV highlighted, and the excision scar shown from division 2.

Open simulation → Micronucleus origin · 100 divisions

Micronuclei & the EGFR×MYCN hub

A different origin: a micronucleus shatters (chromothripsis) and re-ligates into a hybrid circle fusing two non-homologous loci — EGFR (chr7) and MYCN (chr2) — via an inter-chromosomal translocation. The two-fragment hub amplifies under selection, drawn as a concatenated chr7‖chr2 profile with the founder TRA between the chromosomes.
