ecDNA simulations

Three browser-based models of how an extrachromosomal DNA (ecDNA) amplicon is born and evolves — acentric circles carrying oncogenes, followed over 100 cell divisions. Pick a scenario below: neutral drift (no selection), positive selection with rounds of internal rearrangement, and a micronucleus / chromothripsis origin that fuses two chromosomes. Each tab opens that scenario's own write-up and interactive simulation. Built to accompany EpiTracer.
